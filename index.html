A trade firm's size is hard to see from the outside

Most HVAC, plumbing, electrical and roofing shops are private, have no website worth reading and never publish revenue. Brokers show you a firm once it is for sale. Lead lists tell you a firm exists, not whether it is growing.

The public record already shows growth: more work sites on a workers' comp policy, more equipment on the county tax roll, more licenses, more permits. It is spread across five state and county systems. Tradecount pulls it into one record per firm.

Questions buyers ask

Is this a list of firms for sale?

No. It shows growth and activity from the public record. Nothing in it predicts a sale, and we don't broker deals.

Where does the data come from?

Florida DBPR licenses, Florida workers' comp policies, the Florida tangible property tax roll, company filings and county or city permit files where they are free.

Which counties have permits?

Orlando, Miami-Dade County and Cape Coral today. The other ten counties show the five-year state record, and each page says which you are looking at.

Can I export it?

You can download your watchlist of up to 100 firms as a CSV. The full map stays in the portal and is watermarked with your email.
