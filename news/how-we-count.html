How we count

Every number comes from a public record

What we count, where it comes from and how sure we are of each match.

What we count

We count the permits a firm actually pulled. For HVAC we count replacement systems, the change-outs that make up most residential work. For plumbing, electrical and roofing we count every permit issued in that trade, and mark the ones that read as replacement or repair work.

Rankings compare the last twelve months with the twelve before, so a firm's change is measured against itself.

Where it comes from

  • Permits: City of Orlando open permit records and Miami-Dade County building permits. Unincorporated Orange County is on the way.
  • Licenses: the Florida Department of Business and Professional Regulation license files.
  • Crew sites: Florida Division of Workers' Compensation coverage records.
  • Equipment: the Florida Department of Revenue tangible personal property roll.
  • Company records: Florida Division of Corporations filings.

How we match a permit to a firm

Where a permit lists a license number, we use it. Where it lists only names, we match the company and the license qualifier against state license files after cleaning up abbreviations and truncated names. Each firm carries a match grade: high when the license number or both names agree, medium when only one does. Permits we can't match stay out of firm rankings and are counted in area totals as estimates.

What we don't do

We don't look up people, buy private data or guess at who might sell. Tradecount shows growth and activity on the public record, and nothing here predicts a sale.

How often it updates

Permits and licenses refresh monthly. Workers' comp and tax roll records refresh when the state publishes them.
