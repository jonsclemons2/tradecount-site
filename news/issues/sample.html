The Rough-In, issue 14

Orlando's AC change-outs ran 9% ahead of last fall

Monday, November 23, 2026. Five-minute read.

This week in four numbers

HVAC: two mid-size firms took most of the gain

Replacement permits in Orlando rose for the third straight month. Sunbelt Air Services and Lakeshore Air & Heat together pulled 212 of the 1,284, up from 141 a year ago, mostly in 32817 and 32822.

On the map: both firms added a workers' comp location this year, and both now work ZIPs they hadn't touched in 2025.

Plumbing: four new shops in Orange County

Four master plumbers who left larger firms this year pulled their first permits under their own licenses. Two are already busier than their old employers' second crews.

Electrical: panel upgrades doubled in three Miami ZIPs

Service upgrades to 200 amps doubled in 33155, 33142 and 33183. EV chargers and heat pumps are the reason given on most permits.

Roofing: a quiet month after a busy summer

Re-roof permits fell 5% in Miami-Dade, the usual dip after storm season. The top ten roofers held 41% of the work, about the same as last year.

New shops on the record

  • Coquina Plumbing, Orange, first permit Oct 6
  • Banyan Electric, Miami-Dade, first permit Oct 14
  • Heron Cooling, Orange, first permit Oct 22
